    How this works

    Pick a location and describe the system. The estimator pulls hourly historical weather for that spot from the Open-Meteo archive — the ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis, the same dataset used for climate research — and runs every hour of the last decade through a physical model of the array.

    1. Sun position for every hour, from the NOAA solar algorithm.
    2. Irradiance on the panel plane, via the Perez sky-diffuse model.
    3. Cell temperature from air temperature, wind and mounting style.
    4. AC output after temperature derate, system losses and the inverter.

    Because it runs ten separate years, you get the spread between a good year and a poor one — not a single number pretending to be certain.
